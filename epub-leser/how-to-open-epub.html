How to open an EPUB file online

You can read many DRM-free EPUB books directly in a modern web browser. You do not need to install a desktop reader: choose a file from your device and open it in the MOLITOR EPUB Reader.

Open the free EPUB Reader →

Open your first EPUB in three steps

  1. Save the EPUB file. Download a DRM-free .epub file from a source you trust and remember where your browser or device saves it.
  2. Open the reader. Visit the MOLITOR EPUB Reader in a current browser on your computer, phone or tablet.
  3. Select the book. Choose the EPUB file or, on supported desktop browsers, drag it onto the drop area. The reader processes the selected book in your browser.

What file types are supported?

The reader is designed for EPUB files. PDF documents and proprietary Kindle formats such as AZW are not supported by this EPUB tool. DRM-protected books may not open; use files you are legally entitled to access without DRM restrictions.

Do you need to upload your book?

No book upload to a MOLITOR account is required to open a book. The selected EPUB is processed in your browser. The reader may store book details, reading position, bookmarks and other supported reading data in your browser's local storage or IndexedDB. This data is tied to the browser and device profile and can be lost if site data is cleared or the device is reset.

Keep a backup.

Keep your original EPUB files somewhere safe. Use the reader's available export or backup controls for supported reading data. Exporting reading data does not necessarily include the original EPUB files.

Does the reader work offline?

Do not assume full offline availability. The reader may cache parts of its app shell, but some required reading libraries are loaded from a content delivery network. An internet connection may therefore be needed when starting or reloading the app.

Reading on a phone, tablet or Windows PC

Open the reader in an up-to-date browser. On mobile devices, file access and add-to-home-screen options depend on the operating system and browser. On Windows, the web app can be used in a browser; this page does not claim that a separate Microsoft Store desktop package is available.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EPUB Reader free?

Yes. The web reader is free to use. Optional support for independent development is voluntary.

Will my reading progress sync between devices?

Do not expect automatic cloud synchronisation. Reading data is stored locally in the browser unless a clearly labelled feature says otherwise.

Can I open a book protected by DRM?

DRM-protected books may be incompatible. The reader is intended for DRM-free EPUB files that you are entitled to use.

What if my EPUB will not open?

Check that the file has an .epub extension, is not damaged and is not DRM-protected. Try another current browser and keep the original file as a backup.
