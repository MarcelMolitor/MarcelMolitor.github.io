## Description
A free, modern EPUB reader for your browser. Open your own EPUB books without uploading them or creating an account. Enjoy a personal bookshelf, bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress and customizable reading themes. Designed for desktop, tablet and mobile. Your books stay in your browser.
