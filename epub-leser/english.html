How to open an EPUB file

  1. Open the reader and select “Datei auswählen” (choose file), or drop your EPUB onto the page.
  2. Use the arrow buttons or swipe to move between pages.
  3. Open the settings to adjust the font size and reading theme.

How your files are handled

Books are processed locally in your browser rather than uploaded to a server. Recent books may be stored in IndexedDB, while reading settings and bookmarks use localStorage. The reader loads its epub.js and JSZip libraries from jsDelivr. Clear the recent-books list if you use a shared device.

EPUB vs PDF: Which format should you read?

Many EPUB books adapt their text to the screen and font size, whereas PDF usually preserves a fixed page layout. EPUB is useful for long-form reading on smaller screens. This reader opens EPUB files, not PDFs.

Why will my EPUB not open?

Check that the download is complete and the file is a valid EPUB. DRM-protected books may be incompatible. The reader needs access to its library CDN at startup.

EPUB reader: common questions

How do I open an EPUB on Windows?

Open the reader in your browser, select an EPUB file and start reading. No native Windows installation is required.

Can I take notes?

Use the note button in the reading toolbar to save a note linked to your current reading position.

Can the reader open Kindle or PDF files?

The reader supports EPUB, not PDF or proprietary Kindle formats. DRM-protected EPUBs may not work.
