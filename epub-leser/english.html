Private by design

Your book is opened in your browser instead of being uploaded to a reading account or cloud library.

Recent books and reading information may be stored in your browser. Clearing site data can remove this local library, so keep your original EPUB files and export reading data regularly.

What to know

  • DRM-free EPUB files are supported.
  • PDF and proprietary Kindle formats are not supported.
  • DRM-protected EPUBs may not open.
  • The reader loads epub.js and JSZip from jsDelivr, so an internet connection may be needed when starting it.
  • Full offline reading is not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this EPUB reader really free?

Yes. The reader is free to use. Optional support for independent development is voluntary and does not unlock features.

Are my books uploaded?

The selected EPUB is processed in your browser. Recent-book information and reading data may be stored locally in browser storage.

Can I read PDF or Kindle books?

No. This reader is designed for EPUB files. PDF and proprietary Kindle formats are not supported, and DRM-protected EPUB files may be incompatible.

Can I use it on a phone or tablet?

The interface is responsive and can be opened in a modern mobile browser. File selection and installation options depend on your device and browser.

Does it work offline?

Not fully. The reader shell may be cached by a supported browser, but its EPUB libraries are loaded from a content delivery network. Full offline reading is not guaranteed.
