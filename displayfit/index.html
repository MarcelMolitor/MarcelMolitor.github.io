Phone Wallpaper Fitter

Developed in Germany

Create a 1080 × 1920 wallpaper from your own JPEG or PNG on your PC.

Download for Windows
Windows 11 x64 · Version 1.0 · portable, unsigned EXE · German interface
♥ Support this project with PayPal

Enjoy the free app? Support independent development voluntarily.

Create phone wallpapers

The centered crop uses a 9:16 aspect ratio. Transparent areas become white; EXIF orientation is not retained.

Choose a photo up to 36 megapixels and a new filename. Transfer the resulting JPEG to your phone as usual.

Voluntary support

This Windows utility is free, with no subscription or paid unlocks. If you find it useful, you can voluntarily support development. Payment is not required for download or use.
