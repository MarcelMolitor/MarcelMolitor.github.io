Help and troubleshooting (German) · Deutsch

How it works

  1. Open the app and choose a JPEG or PNG image up to 36 megapixels.
  2. Choose a new file name. The app reduces JPEG quality and, if necessary, resolution until the copy is smaller than 1,000,000 bytes.
  3. Open the copy and check orientation and readability. Transparent areas become white. Camera metadata and EXIF orientation are not retained.

How to reduce an image to under 1 MB

For application forms and email attachments, file size matters more than pixel count alone. Reduce JPEG quality or resolution and check that text remains readable. Save the result under a different filename to preserve the original. PNG transparency is replaced with white. The portable Windows executable is unsigned; verify its source and do not bypass security warnings.
