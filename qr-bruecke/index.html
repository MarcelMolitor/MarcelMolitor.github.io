How to send a website from your computer to Android or iPhone

  1. Copy the URL from your browser address bar.
  2. Paste it into QR Link Transfer and generate the code.
  3. Scan with your phone camera and check the destination before opening it.

Do I need Bluetooth or a messaging app?

No. The QR code transfers the address visually, without pairing devices. Your phone still needs network access to the destination.

Why does a localhost link fail?

Localhost refers to the phone itself, not the PC. This utility transfers addresses; it does not host websites or provide a network tunnel.
